Medical Air Technology

Critical ventilation systems and turnkey solutions for healthcare and life sciences

Medical Air Technology (MAT) supplies specialist critical ventilation systems for the NHS, private healthcare providers, universities and research facilities. We are a world-class company, growing and innovating every day to better serve our customers.


Specialised ventilation is provided in areas such as operating departments, critical care and isolation facilities, in line with HTM 03-01 Specialised ventilation for healthcare premises (2021).


Critical ventilation ensures compliance with the quality assurance requirements of items processed in pharmacies and sterile services departments. Its use also protects staff from harmful organisms and toxic substances.

Whether you want a total turnkey installation or our MCP (MAT Core Package), we have the solution you need.

MAT FM, our facilities management division, provides ongoing maintenance of critical ventilation systems for safe, energy-efficient operation and compliance with regulatory guidelines.

At MAT and MAT FM, we put patient and operator safety right at the heart of everything we do, whether designing safe environments for cutting-edge research into dangerous pathogens or reducing incidence of surgical site infection by engineering ultraclean environments for complex surgery.
